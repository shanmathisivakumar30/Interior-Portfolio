The Human bit

I’m Shanmathi, an interior designer and architect, and a constellation of places, projects, people, and things I’ve paused to notice.

I like spaces that take a deep breath. A little less clutter, a little more intention, and a bright pop of colour.

Away from drawings, I’m usually collecting words, adjusting a recipe, or turning a passing curiosity into a small research project. Apparently, making things feel right follows me home.
