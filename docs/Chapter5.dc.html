01 / The working day

Care begins with the room.

206 Stories explores a warmer spatial language for healthcare. Sculpted forms, navy accents, and a restrained material palette bring clarity and character to the reception, corridors, and consultation rooms.

Light reveals texture, softens thresholds, and gives each space its own rhythm. The design makes room for the person behind the appointment.

A place for care, and the stories that arrive with it.
