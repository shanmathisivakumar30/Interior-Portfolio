01 / Light, within!

Let the room speak to you

Light, Within is a home shaped around the slow movement of daylight. Rather than placing nature at its edges, the house draws it inward. Courtyards interrupt rooms, trees rise through voids, water slips alongside circulation, and screens turn sunlight into shifting patterns across stone and plaster.

The house is not built around a view of nature. It is built around living within it.

Light, within and everywhere!
